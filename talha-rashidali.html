About

I design and build websites that load quickly, read clearly, and work for everyone who lands on them. Most of my days are spent in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, turning rough sketches and half-formed ideas into interfaces people can actually use.

I care about the parts nobody notices when they are done right: keyboard navigation, sensible colour contrast, layouts that hold up on a five-year-old phone, and pages that stay under a second to first paint.

Right now I take on freelance and contract work — landing pages, portfolios, dashboards, and small product front-ends.
